The TrialNN family

The trial-solution network of TrialNN_PDE_int is a NetworkAnsatz under HardConstraintLifting. The slab's field is the network's field, projected so that it vanishes at both ends of xspan and at the bottom of the slab, plus a transfinite interpolant of the slab's data: the problem's boundary function at the two ends, and the initial condition at the first slab and the previous slab's top after it. The slab's interior weak form alone is the residual.

The boundary function is met at the two ends at every time. The bottom datum is stored at the space nodes of the ansatz's quadrature rule and is met at those nodes. Between two nodes the interpolant takes the linear blend of their two values, and outside the outer nodes it takes the boundary function's value at that end at the slab's start.

The family carries no momentum between slabs, on purpose: the coupling has no unknowns of its own, no surface term and no load at the bottom of a slab.

The family is declined by the method and is kept for comparison: it converges on Wave only under a variable-projection solve, with the error of the unconverged run, and under no solve on a Klein–Gordon layer whose density depends on the position (K47 in KNOWN_ISSUES.md). The lifting on a TensorBSplineAnsatz is not part of that decision.

The old PINN pre-training branch and PINN_for_init.jl are the fit method PINNTraining, and the old OGA2D seed is OrthogonalGreedy. PINNTraining draws its sample points from its own generator, rng = Xoshiro(1234) by default. Each example prints the run's stats and claims nothing about them.

using MultisymplecticIntegrators

problem = Wave.problem(timespan = (0.0, 0.2), timestep = 0.1, xstep = 0.05)
ansatz = NetworkAnsatz(S = 4, mode = :all, xspan = (0.0, 1.0), degree = 2, ncells = (2, 4))
method = SpaceTimeGalerkin(ansatz, HardConstraintLifting();
    initial_guess = FitTo(PreviousSlab(); method = PINNTraining()))
result = integrate(problem, method)
result.stats
(iterations = [28, 1], residuals = [1.4864348354884176e-15, 6.78352075319967e-30], initial_residuals = [1.8511431718002385, 1.9389373476122677e-29], converged = Bool[1, 1])
method = SpaceTimeGalerkin(ansatz, HardConstraintLifting();
    initial_guess = FitTo(InitialCondition(); method = OrthogonalGreedy(Nw = 10, Nb = 10)))
result = integrate(problem, method)
result.stats
(iterations = [66, 133], residuals = [0.0039451592791859445, 0.0003313681836349174], initial_residuals = [0.4493810199533337, 0.5620071957278805], converged = Bool[0, 0])
