The box scheme

BoxScheme is the Preissmann box scheme of a density's first-order form K z_t + L z_x = ∇S(z), with z = (u, v, w): the baseline that every other method of the package is compared against. It is a method of its own, not a SpaceTimeGalerkin, and integrate advances it like any other method. Every row of the form is evaluated at the centre of a box of the output grid, which is second order in the time step and in the space step and satisfies the discrete multisymplectic conservation law of the form.

The resolution is the problem's own, one box per timestep and xstep, and the state lives at the nodes of the output grid. Wave, VectorWave and SineGordon are in the class of densities the scheme admits; a density outside it, such as LinearTransport's, is refused when the integrator is built.

using MultisymplecticIntegrators

problem = Wave.problem(timespan = (0.0, 0.2), timestep = 0.1, xstep = 0.05)
result = integrate(problem, BoxScheme())
result.stats
(iterations = [5, 3], residuals = [8.243744718428647e-15, 7.555764089057643e-15], initial_residuals = [9.96289557978721, 10.415456962721485], converged = Bool[1, 1])

The scheme's energy, which energy_error reports, is the density's Hamiltonian at the midpoints of the boxes, summed with the weights of box_quadrature_weights.
