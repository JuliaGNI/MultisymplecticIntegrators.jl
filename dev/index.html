MultisymplecticIntegrators

Documentation for MultisymplecticIntegrators.

Multi-symplectic schemes for Lagrangian partial differential equations in one space dimension: the Lagrangian problems with their exact solutions, a space-time quadrature, and the shell that drives an integrator over the slabs of the time span.

The package is mid-rebuild

The problem layer, the space-time quadrature and the integrator shell are in place, and the integrator families come back one at a time. A family that has not returned yet is unavailable in this version; the old stack stays at 294f023.
