Row 1's tests check the wave energy alone. The sine-Gordon and transport energies are checked only for their element type, by "a Float32 SineGordon problem evaluates its energy in Float32" of test/problems/lagrangian_problem.jl and "a Float32 problem evaluates its energy in Float32" of test/problems/linear_transport.jl. Row 3's multiplier_stride is gone: every trace function carries a multiplier, the corners excepted. Row 7 needs no start-up, because the step is a one-step form, and its velocity is the ansatz's ∂u/∂t̂ / h. Row 19's carry is each coupling's; the lifting of the TrialNN family carries none, and the other couplings' carries are tested in test/couplings/coefficient_restriction.jl and test/couplings/weak_multipliers.jl.
